About Me

Balancing user empathy with evocative visual craft.

I am an Interaction Design student at UPES (University of Petroleum and Energy Studies). My design philosophy bridges pragmatic problem-solving with artistic depth—whether that is solving real-world kitchen inventory challenges with smart IoT interfaces or celebrating Indian heritage through the 9 Rasas.

I believe every detail matters—from micro-interactions in a mobile app to typographic cadence in brand collateral. Design should not just function; it should evoke emotion and resonate.
