Designer Profile

Balancing artistic emotion with pragmatic engineering.

I am an Interaction Design student at UPES (University of Petroleum and Energy Studies, Dehradun). My work lives at the intersection of bold graphic communication, deep cultural heritage, and modern user-centered interaction design.

Whether formulating a sports broadcast brand identity, reviving the Navarasas through botanical digital paintings, or tackling household food waste with hardware IoT displays, I believe every detail must have intent.

01

Discover & Question

02

03

Craft & Iterate

Pixel-perfect execution in Illustrator, Photoshop, and Figma with meticulous craft.

04

Deliver & Storytell

Presenting work with rationale, context, and clear real-world resonance.
